A bit about me

I'm a 3D Technical Artist based in Ontario, Canada, with over 10 years of experience across VFX and feature animation.

I've worked on hero characters for films like Maleficent: Mistress of Evil, Rumble, and Fireheart. My work sits at the intersection of art and code: cloth and hair simulation, character rigging, procedural modeling, and the custom tools that make complex 3D production possible.

I'm equally comfortable sculpting a rig as I am writing a PySide2 tool. That hybrid skill set lets me solve problems other artists can't, and build tools other developers wouldn't think to make.
