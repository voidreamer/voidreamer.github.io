About

Art meets engineering

I'm a 3D Technical Artist based in Ontario, Canada, with over a decade in VFX and feature animation. I specialize in the space where artistry and technical problem-solving intersect.

My work spans character FX (cloth, hair, muscle simulation), rigging, lighting, and procedural workflows using USD, Solaris, and Bifrost. I build tools that empower artists to focus on creativity instead of fighting their software.

I've contributed to feature films including Maleficent: Mistress of Evil, Rumble, and Fireheart, working on everything from hero character simulations to pipeline-level automation.
